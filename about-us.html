About Pinnacle First

Independent Eyes. Clear Decisions.

We help procurement and QA teams verify vendor quality and monitor delivery—without needing to be on every shop floor themselves.

Our Story

Built to Close the Gap Between Order and Delivery

Pinnacle First Inspection was founded in 2018 to address a costly gap for industrial buyers: the difference between what a purchase order specifies and what leaves a vendor’s shop floor. Headquartered in Gurgaon, India, we work alongside procurement and quality teams that need reliable field support at vendor facilities.

What began as third-party inspection support has grown into a coordinated practice covering inspection, expediting, vendor audits and marine cargo supervision. Our work is built around the realities of Oil & Gas and Petrochemical fabrication, where quality issues and missed milestones can have significant project consequences.

Our inspectors and expediting engineers support vendor facilities across India and international project locations. We work against the client’s purchase order, approved drawings, codes and Inspection & Test Plan—and report findings with evidence.
