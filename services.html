What We Do

Four Disciplines. One Purpose.

We connect quality verification with schedule awareness—from vendor capability and fabrication stages through testing and shipment readiness.

01 / Inspection

Third-Party Inspection

Independent inspectors witness manufacturing, testing and final acceptance at vendor facilities, against the purchase order, approved drawings, project specifications and applicable codes.

  • Raw material and incoming inspection
  • In-process / stage inspection
  • Final and pre-shipment inspection
  • NDT witnessing (RT, UT, MT, PT)
  • Hydrostatic and pneumatic test witnessing
  • Dimensional and visual verification
02 / Expediting

Vendor Expediting

Expediting engineers follow vendor progress against the approved schedule, raise delays and bottlenecks early, and follow up on agreed recovery actions.

  • Desk and site progress monitoring
  • Manufacturing schedule tracking
  • Expediting status reporting
  • Delay risk and bottleneck flagging
  • Shipping and documentation coordination
  • Vendor corrective-action follow-up
03 / Audit & Surveillance

Vendor Audit

Assess vendor quality systems and manufacturing capability before award, then maintain visibility during production.

  • Pre-award capability audits
  • QMS and ISO 9001 compliance review
  • Ongoing shop surveillance visits
  • Sub-vendor and sub-tier audits
  • Welding procedure and welder qualification review
  • Corrective / preventive action follow-up
04 / Marine

Loading & Unloading Supervision

Surveyors supervise cargo loading or discharge, documenting the condition, quantity and records associated with the operation.

  • Loading and discharge supervision
  • Draft survey and quantity verification
  • Condition and damage survey
  • Sealing and tally verification
  • Bill of lading and cargo document review
  • Ship / shore interface coordination

How We Work
